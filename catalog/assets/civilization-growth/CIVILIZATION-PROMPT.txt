Use case: stylized-concept. Create one actual final-frame direction study for a Chinese motion artwork about 火种 / 薪火相传 / the continuing creativity of humanity. WIDE landscape 16:9 composition, intended at 1920 x 1080. No video-player UI, no title, no slogan, no border, no arrows.
Art direction: precise antique copperplate engravings and delicate scientific pen drawings in black and warm dark gray on a flat pale gray-green paper (#e2e3dd), dry ink, tiny etched hatch marks, coherent fine lines, mostly light airy line work with occasional black solid masses. All subjects belong to the same beautifully composed encyclopedic print. Elegant detailed engraving, not cartoon icons, not photorealistic cutouts, not sepia or brown.
The picture conveys FIRE -> SEEDS -> TOOLS -> WRITING -> MACHINES -> EXPLORATION by the content of neighboring illustrations. It is an imaginative visual atlas, not a literal claim of historical chronology.
COMPOSITION IS THE MAIN REQUIREMENT: densely tessellated, tightly interlocked arrangements of about 30 different subjects, with several large anchor illustrations and many small technical detail drawings occupying the gaps. The content extends almost edge to edge within a slim 3% margin. No large empty center, no isolated evenly spaced stickers, no rigid cards, no equal-sized tile grid, no blank horizontal strips. Upright and horizontal drawings fit around each other's contours. Varied scale, strong ordered rhythm, clear individual silhouettes separated by narrow paper gaps. The center must be as rich and tightly packed as the edges.
Left quarter: a powerful medium-sized engraving of two HUMAN HANDS using a wooden hand drill to make fire on a fireboard, friction smoke curling up and a tiny flame at the point of contact. Nearby fit an obsidian stone blade, a clay pot with a sectional diagram, four seed kernels and a wheat ear, a small plow, a compact root-and-sprout diagram, a small fire silhouette. At upper left-middle fit FOUR small different flowers in a compact neat two-by-two botanical study, short stems, all the same visual size; they occupy only one small region, never the center.
Middle half: a large beautifully detailed wooden loom with a cloth emerging, a separate water wheel and stream channel, a tiny pottery-wheel profile, narrow vertical bamboo writing slips, four Chinese movable type blocks bearing exactly 山 川 日 月, an open book, a vintage printing press, a horizontal exploded mechanism with interlocking gear wheels and a flywheel, a pendulum mechanism. Interleave small drawings of threads, gear profiles, seed cross sections and a lens cross section to fill small gaps without labels.
Right quarter and lower center: an armillary sphere at upper right, a small sundial, a large telescope on its fork stand, a short steam engine cutaway with piston/flywheel, a slender bridge elevation, a sailing ship side profile, a space capsule cutaway, a small satellite with two solar arrays, and a slender ascending rocket at the far right with a fine engraved flame/plume that visually rhymes with the first little fire. Small orbit arcs and star charts tuck into residual gaps.
Design the whole page as one sophisticated, densely composed engraving atlas about human ingenuity handed onward. Let fire-making, cultivation, making vessels, recording knowledge, moving mechanisms and reaching the stars all remain immediately recognizable to a school student. No giant human portraits, no crowds, no glowing neon, no colorful flames, no decorative flowers dominating the page, no brand marks, no unreadable pseudo-text. Except the four movable-type characters, no lettering at all. Do not leave the bottom half empty.
